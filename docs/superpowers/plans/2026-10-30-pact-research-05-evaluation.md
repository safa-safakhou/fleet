# PACT Metrics and Experiments Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use `superpowers:executing-plans`, inline without delegation. Checkboxes require a covering implementation assignment.

**Goal:** Measure accuracy, useful PACT outcomes, resources and recovery with auditable definitions and defensible comparisons.

**Architecture:** Versioned deterministic evaluators read immutable authorized evidence snapshots, then produce metric values and comparison artifacts. Promptfoo and DSPy call Fleet's broker; MLflow receives a replaceable sanitized metadata projection.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript/PostgreSQL, existing evaluation/comparison modules, Promptfoo, isolated Python/DSPy/MLflow, fast-check and Hypothesis.

**Spec:** [Design §§8–11](../specs/2026-10-30-pact-research-engine-design.md); [master constraints](2026-10-30-pact-research-engine.md). Depends on O1–O6, R4–R5 and M1–M4.

## Global Constraints

- No real-provider/game activity or automatic promotion. Fake/recorded synthetic providers are the default; future real calls need explicit resource authorization.
- Null, censored, unavailable and unknown remain distinct from zero. Known tokens never imply all usage is known.
- Metric time windows are half-open `[start,end)`. Published snapshots do not mutate when late events arrive.
- Private observations retain 30 days; default artifacts are encrypted, at most 1 MiB/chunk. No private content in Promptfoo/MLflow logs or unmanaged exports; pages default 200/max 500.
- Agent turns in one shared world are not independent trials. Evaluator/oracle data cannot enter acting context, memory or optimization held-out inputs.

## Review Focus

1. Aggregates over more than 200 calls disagree with a capped drilldown — A1.
2. WAIT follows a committed action, or a receipt precedes completion — A1/A2.
3. Unknown usage, late settlement or incomplete observations make a cheap-looking candidate misleading — A1/A2.
4. World interference or selective missing results creates a false winner — A2.
5. Framework retries, teacher/judge calls or train/test overlap escape the ledger/evaluation boundary — A3/A4.
