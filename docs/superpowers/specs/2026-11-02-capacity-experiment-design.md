## Approach and tradeoffs

Use one long-lived Fleet runtime, shared DB pool, 24 long-lived manifest-bound Temporal workers with manual delivery, and up to six independent Nemotron 4B servers behind a local single-attempt router. Admit work at 2, 4 and at most 6 concurrent calls after known settled results at the current level; require measured whole-turn latency with forecast headroom for the next level and at least 35% available memory. Keep one active turn per binding. Each backend has one inference slot; preserve identical model weights, precision and version.

This replaces the initial one-server/eight-slot design after actual attempt 02 proved that pinned Ollama 0.40.11 forces `nemotron_h` to one slot. No successor window is sealed.

The router forwards exact input and streamed output bytes, never retries or falls back, refuses excess before dispatch, and records metadata/request digests without payload logs. Failed or disconnected replicas cannot be reused until the router is recreated; disconnect alone does not prove inference has stopped. Native EventIntake retains category/group task keys, cursor/dedup/quiet/resync/deadline semantics; do not collapse unrelated deadlines into one invented global key. New tasks inherit ownership atomically. Ordinary workers cannot claim them. Manual worker delivery means all24 workers remain ready, but the operator sends at most one new wake per binding and at most the current global slot count. Every native execution still has fresh claim/fence/turn and exact scoped evidence.

Select eligible tasks with deadline urgency and fair actor rotation, preserving independent per-binding eligibility. Record trigger→ingest→eligible→dispatch→provider→decision→receipt timings separately. Skip neither queued nor refused work in metric populations. Native deadline checks remain authoritative; a stale event is a measured scheduling miss, not permission to replay it. Definite model/proposal failures are retained and can quarantine that binding; unknown usage/commands, failed capture, service/process loss or resource/clock/window breach close admission globally. No automatic retry of failed tasks.
