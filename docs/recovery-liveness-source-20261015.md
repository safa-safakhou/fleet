## Durable recovery design

Owner intent remains the cohort's explicit running/paused/stopped state. Automatic Fleet faults no longer write a cohort pause. Prospective turns have a durable operation record linked to the existing worker, generation, fence, deadline and ledger. Historical blocked turns are not backfilled or automatically reviewed. States distinguish execution, next-slot retry, receipt reconciliation, owner exposure/auth gates and completed recovery. Original anchors are retained; recovery never runs a burst of missed inference turns.

Certified model adapters journal a dispatch checkpoint before sending work. Provable nonexecution retains the full allocation/call counter with `not_dispatched`, null actual usage and no invented zero bill. A completed CLI answer with valid final usage keeps that usage even when its answer is malformed. Incomplete/ambiguous event streams remain unknown. Reported overages retain measured totals. Verified late usage is additional evidence, not an automatic unknown-allocation refund or review.

Genuinely unknown inference remains a scoped owner exposure gate. For finite shared Codex allowances, unreviewed unknown consumption also gates new paid calls against that shared allowance: 40,000 is an internal reservation, not a provider-enforced ceiling. Continuous mode does not acquire a new aggregate cap. No unlimited paid retry or model health probe is introduced. Choosing broader automatic unknown-cost continuation requires an explicit exposure policy or enforceable provider bound; neither is silently enabled.

Fleet's CLI uses a kernel-held lock and an independent process-tree deadline, so worker death cannot permanently strand a new directory lock. The independent cap is at most 95 seconds; the existing 90-second model/turn signal remains. Legacy unverifiable directory locks are exposed for ownership review, never blindly removed. Cleanup errors retain completed usage.

Game mutations journal the exact original ID/action before dispatch. Dispatch markers and expiry recovery lock the same turn, preventing a stale worker racing recovery. A tracked command proven unsent is archived as `COMMAND_NOT_DISPATCHED`, source `not_dispatched`, not as a fabricated game receipt. Submitted/uncertain commands are only reconciled against their original receipt, with leased backoff capped at five minutes. A missing receipt remains unresolved; no replacement ID or blind replay is issued. Receipt resolution restores eligibility only after other usage, credential, configuration and owner-state blockers clear.

The opt-in PACT refresh contract uses an independent pre-saved 32-byte proof and an atomically encrypted, product/client/parent-bound result. Fleet saves the encrypted proof and recovery job in one transaction, recovers under per-account singleflight and ciphertext/fence/account CAS, and atomically saves credentials/completes recovery. Retry delays are bounded, expiry/protocol downgrade/revocation require same-account reconnection. Legacy uncertain refreshes without a proof are not replayed. One idle account can reconnect while unrelated accounts run. Reconnection cannot clear unrelated turn/configuration blockers; original bindings are verified. Initial enrollment still requires all accounts. Resuming an anchored cohort expresses owner intent while blocked accounts remain excluded.

Supervision survives database/iteration errors, bounds database statements/locks/idle transactions, exposes low-disk readiness, and runs expiry/recovery independently from long turns and new admissions. Recovery is permitted without new dispatch on low disk; history/backups are not deleted. Claim capacity is filtered before the earliest candidate. Durable budget/dependency waits expose their wake condition. Maintenance uses a five-minute renewable hold, separate from owner intent; release/expiry requires natural turn/credential/callback quiescence and never clears a manual pause. Worker shutdown grace covers bounded operations.

PACT in-app execution is separate from external Fleet. Jobs have renewable unique acquisition ownership and nonoverlapping polling. Provider calls are bounded even if an adapter ignores cancellation. Atomic operation-phase checks fence late workers before provider/tool dispatch. Late results cannot execute tools; usage may be retained as evidence. Known safe pre-dispatch failures and orphan operations proven to have no game effects can recover within original limits. Unknown provider/game outcomes stay scoped gates, with durable reservation evidence; enrollment allowances are not replenished. Shared kingdom write leases and canonical command receipts remain authoritative.

Only future world pauses explicitly marked operational are eligible for automatic recovery. Repeated database/tick-history checks must agree with committed time/sequence, and the pause must age at least five seconds. Recovery schedules the next minute from current wall time without advancing committed game time or replaying offline economic ticks. Manual and legacy pauses remain held. No game economy, authority, cancellation policy, model, budget or experiment is changed.
