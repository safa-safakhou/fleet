## Main gaps

1. **Writing:** modern models omit structured updates while sometimes narrating memorization. A legacy non-thinking control used both calls rereading state and never attempted a write; that is not a rejected or impossible write.
2. **Retrieval/delivery:** a saved missed-delivery memory ranked 7 th and was absent from the next five-item context along with its outcome source. Repeated stock observations crowded them out; exact replay matches the delivered IDs.
3. **Query/candidates:** focused queries help, but Laya cannot recover sources excluded from its top two candidates. Relevance selection is not claim verification.
4. **Reasoning/receipts:** one valid response treated 60 iron as satisfying an 80-iron reserve and claimed acquired timber without a receipt. Structure and citation ownership do not prove truth or arithmetic.
5. **Outcomes:** dependable survival, diplomacy, obligation fulfilment and long-term planning under new engines are unproved. No fair architecture winner is established.
6. **Live integration/scale:** exact PACT protocol/source/model pairing and a small bounded end-to-end run remain before expansion. F6 and a new population have extra prerequisites.
7. **Eligibility:** execution-disable and old actor blockers are separate. A passing harness does not review unknowns or resume the original 20 agents.
