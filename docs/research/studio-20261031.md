Original-run consent and real O2 dependency checks protect source expansion. Known deadlines and authorization invalidation clear private copies; stale requests cannot refill a newer view. Remote revocation is discovered on the next focus/interaction/heartbeat check.

No retained evidence was used. Owned fixture processes/containers were stopped; ignored evidence was preserved.
