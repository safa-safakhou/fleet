Only local pre-HTTP connection refusal permits one bounded immediate refresh retry. Two confirmed nonexecutions retain old credentials and use the original next scheduled turn; ambiguous 502/lost response and explicit OAuth rejection require same-account reconnect. Newer grants and original pending/usage history are protected by shared locks, credential CAS and fences. Current unused server-token metadata is never a retry grant. OAuth callback nonces carry a generation; old/missing-generation nonces are retained as uncertain and require a fresh authorized reconnect flow. A busy callback leaves its nonce pending.
